f00 · open

Come unfinished.

We are not hiring a resume. We are looking for people who still see the seam in the thing everyone else calls finished.

This is not just coding

Code is one tool. So is ink. So is a camera. So is a zine folded wrong on purpose. So is a song that almost holds together. If it is a project — something you can put in the world and stand next to — it can live here. Software. Print. Sound. Film. Installation. Whatever cuts.

What we already know

Nothing made has ever been identical. Nothing real has ever been uniform. The only absolute is mathematics. Everything else is a throw of light through imperfect matter onto a surface that was never flat.

Design culture chases the pixel until the vision bleeds out. We do the other thing. We pull the skin open. We show the organs. We leave the anti-uniformity in — natural, organic, entropic — because that is the world, and the world is the only honest brief.

Who we want

Artists. Coders. Visionaries. Musicians. Bands. Filmmakers. Writers who hate clean type. People who make zines at 3 a.m. People who take machines apart to see why they hum. People who already work at the edge and refuse to sand it down.

If you only ship what looks approved, this is not your room. If you still get sick at the fake polish, come closer.

What fits

Anything that could sit on the hub without lying — a tool, a print run, a score, a film, a show, a page that hurts a little to look at in a good way. Cutting edge without the costume. Alive first. Readable enough to use. Soul intact.

We expose the innards of what passes for standard. Routine is a costume. Under it: grain, scar, accident, species you recognize and specimens you never confuse.

How to show up

Bring the work. Not a pitch deck. The thing itself — repo, scan, tape, reel, proof it exists. Tell us what you opened and what you refused to close. If it rhymes with the visual mantra, we will hear it.

Open a door on github.com/f00-sh · or put f00 in the subject and send the scar.

Leave the math for the math

Everywhere else, people sand the world until it looks like a spreadsheet. We want the opposite. Perfect anti-uniformity. Hands in the guts. Projects that still bleed a little when you pick them up.

Come unfinished.
Stay honest.
