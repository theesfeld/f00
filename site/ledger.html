The law of the books

Every movement of value into or out of the collective is a line here. Kinds: gift, expense, physical sale, transfer, note. No salary. No wage. No “contractor lifestyle” line that is payroll in costume. Program spend only — albums, books, zines, servers, supplies, postage — and even-split of unrestricted gifts when that is how the table divides bread.

Source of truth is the file ledger.json in git. If it is not in the ledger, it did not happen. Open books are our practice now; they are not a claim that we are already a registered non-profit or that gifts are tax-deductible.
