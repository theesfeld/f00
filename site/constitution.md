# f00 constitution

**How we bind ourselves.**

Public living document. Not a company charter. Not a list of punishments.
The door is [who](https://f00.sh/who). The path toward a formal public-benefit
shell is the [plan](https://f00.sh/plan). This file is the full practice.

HTML: <https://f00.sh/constitution>

---

## Preamble

f00 is a collective of people who build with machines and with hands —
code, print, sound, film, whatever cuts. Not a vendor. Not a brand deck.
A room of people who make things that refuse to lie about the world. We
do this for love. We project the real world onto the display. We leave
the math for the math. We come unfinished.

## I · Collective, not corporation

We are not a software company. We are not a membership club with ladders.
The face of f00 is **projects** — tools, zines, albums, film, pages —
sitting next to each other without sanding the soul off. People still
decide. Titles that rank people over projects do not live here: no owners,
heads, leads, masters, or gods. Only **stewards** of the work they put down.

## II · Stewards and the vote

If you steward at least one **released** project under f00, you hold a
vote. **One person, one vote** — no matter how many projects you already
carry. Having one in does not mean the next one walks in. A yes is for
that project, that time — not a lifetime pass. A no is not exile. Bring
it again when it is sharper, or make something else.

## III · How a project lands

**Propose.** Anyone may bring work — or a sharp outline — that could live
on the hub without lying. Contact: tj@f00.sh or @lameserver on X. What it
is, why it fits, who would steward it.

**Open.** The proposal is written into the open record where voters can
all see it. Stewards get a clear window to vote yes or no. Votes are by
person, recorded in the open. Simple majority of votes cast decides.
Chat rooms may exist for talk. They are not the ledger. The vote lives
where anyone can read it later.

**Land or not.** Yes → the project may take a seat on the hub when it has
a real release. No → the idea is free; try again later or elsewhere.

## IV · Open to the world

Every f00 project is MIT-licensed where it is code, and open to the world
either way. Free to read, copy, change, and share. No payments to use the
work. Digital drops stay free. Physical things (vinyl, books, zines) may
exist only to cover cost and feed the collective pool so more work can
ship — not so anyone gets rich. No closed editions dressed as freedom.
No paywalls. No “owners” of culture — only stewards of what they put down.

## V · Money is bread, not rent

We do not monetize this directly. No funnel. No cup on the sidewalk. We
do not beg. We do not panhandle. The practice is quiet: make the thing,
put it down, keep walking.

If someone chooses to give, that is allowed — **one collective pool only**,
never per project — and gifts are shared like bread, not a tip jar for a
founder. Optional support: <https://f00.sh/donate>. Every movement of
value is on the open ledger (<https://f00.sh/ledger>, `ledger.json`) —
no payroll, no private books. If it is not in the ledger, it did not happen.

The pool funds program work: albums, books, zines, servers, domains,
materials, postage — more released work. No one takes a profit cut. No
equity. Stewards do not get rich; the table gets larger.

## VI · Public-benefit shell (aspiration)

We are **not** a registered public charity or 501(c)(3) today. Do not
treat gifts as tax-deductible. We are working toward a formal
public-benefit shell so we can receive and pass support without becoming
a shop. We will not claim that status until it is true and published on
the hub. Living path: <https://f00.sh/plan>. Until then, and after, the
work stands on love alone — and this constitution still holds.

## VII · Mindset — the world is a projection

**Perfection is a math problem.** Uniformity only lives in equations.
Outside mathematics, nothing is 100% straight, 100% crisp, 100% the same
as last time. Pattern without clones.

**Order lives inside disorder.** Chaos is not a flat smear. A body under
skin is organs, not noise. Some edges stay sharp. Some dissolve.

**Every object is a throw.** Logo, card, letter, field: each is light
through a thing, through a lens, onto a screen. Not layout jitter dressed
up as life.

**Letterpress, not laser.** Readable first. Alive second. Both required.
Perfect anti-uniformity — natural, organic, entropic — because that is
the world.

**Usable zen.** Entropy is not an excuse for mess. The band is narrow on
purpose: enough variation that the eye believes the world, never so much
that you cannot read, aim, or rest.

## VIII · Methodology — how we build

- **Release is the card.** Only released work sits on the hub grid.
- **One aesthetic spine.** Shared theme from the hub.
- **Catalog is truth.** Project list and links live in one open file.
- **Open process.** Proposals, votes, money, and formation status are
  public enough to audit. Secret power is a bug.
- **Discipline over costume.** Cutting edge without the cosplay.

## IX · Who may walk in

Artists. Coders. Visionaries. Musicians. Bands. Filmmakers. Printers.
Whoever cuts — if it is a project you can stand next to. This is not just
coding.

## X · What we refuse

Manufactured perfection. Identical throws. Razor edges as religion.
Metronome motion. Flat blur as “organic.” Company cosplay. Membership
ladders. Selling the lifestyle. Owners. Heads. Leads. Masters. Gods.
Per-project tip jars. Private books next to a public ledger. Paywalls
dressed as community. Closed source “for sustainability.” Discord as the
only vote record. Claiming charity status we have not earned.

## XI · If we ever end

Any formal shell we form **cannot** be dissolved for profit of any kind —
not for stewards, founders, donors, or anyone else. No residual as equity,
dividend, or private take. On dissolution or liquidation, after lawful
debts, **all remaining funds and property** go only to other public
charities (organizations described as 501(c)(3) when that is the path),
either by transferring the work to another such body or by liquidating and
donating every leftover coin. Preferred residual homes (more to be decided
in the open): **Sisters of the Road**, **Food Not Bombs** (or a qualifying
501(c)(3) host that holds for that mission), and others we name before any
wind-down. Never a private pocket. This is non-negotiable in bylaws and
articles if we file.

## XII · How this document lives

This constitution is public and versioned with the hub. It may change when
stewards of released projects agree, in the open, that the bond needs to
grow. Legal bylaws of any future public-benefit shell must not gut this
culture; they may only dress it for the state. Culture leads. Paperwork
follows — or we do not file. Dissolution-for-profit language is never
weakened.

## Seal

Project the real world onto the display.  
Leave the math for the math.  
Come unfinished. Do it for love.
