f00 · visual mantra

The world is a projection.

Not a page that loads. A throw of light through imperfect things onto a surface that was never flat.

Perfection is a math problem

Uniformity only lives in equations. Outside mathematics, nothing is 100% straight, 100% crisp, 100% the same as last time. Snowflakes, fingerprints, zebra stripes, the drip of a brush: pattern without clones. Species you recognize; specimens you never confuse.

Order lives inside disorder

Chaos is not a flat smear. A body under skin is organs, not noise. Emulsion can be nearly straight on the film and still arrive bent by light. Some edges stay sharp. Some dissolve. Straightness can survive in places — never everywhere at once, and never by force of a single soft filter.

Every object is a throw

Logo, card, rule, letter, field: each is light through a thing, through a lens, onto a screen. Each has its own seat in the tray — its own gate, keystone, focus, lamp. Not one shared wind moving petals. Not layout jitter dressed up as life. A view of a natural thing, projected onto the display.

Letterpress, not laser

Hand-set type knows the spacer’s hand: kerning that wanders, word spaces that breathe, ink that is almost solid, sorts that sit a hair off square. Close to a line. Never a CAD baseline. Readable first. Alive second. Both required.

Usable zen

Entropy is not an excuse for mess. The band is narrow on purpose: enough variation that the eye believes the world, never so much that you cannot read, aim, or rest. Beauty that works. Work that looks like it grew.

What we refuse

Manufactured perfection. Identical throws. Razor edges as religion. Metronome motion. Flat blur as “organic.” Anything that pretends the screen is more pure than the room it sits in.

Project the real world onto the display.
Leave the math for the math.
