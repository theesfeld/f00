# f00 plan — toward a public-benefit shell

**Not legal advice.** Public working plan. We are **not** a registered
non-profit or 501(c)(3) today. Gifts are **not** tax-deductible. We are
trying to become a formal public-benefit body that still obeys the
[constitution](https://f00.sh/constitution). When status is real, we will
say so clearly on this page and on [donate](https://f00.sh/donate).

HTML: <https://f00.sh/plan>

---

## Why bother

So gifts and grants can land in a shared pool that funds open work —
tools, albums, books, zines, film, sound, machines — without private books
or a founder tip jar. So we can report cleanly if a donor needs formal
structure. So the culture survives contact with money.

Why not rush: paperwork is tension with “out of the mall.” Form without
discipline and you get a company with softer language. We would rather
stay pure and slow than claim a costume.

## What must not break

Whatever shell we choose, the [constitution](https://f00.sh/constitution)
still binds practice:

- collective, not vendor — stewards, no owners / heads / leads
- one person, one vote among stewards of released projects
- MIT (or freer) for code; free digital for media
- one pool; open ledger; no payroll default
- physical sales only to recycle cost into more work
- no claim of charity status until true and published
- **never dissolve for private profit** — residual only to other 501(c)(3)s

## If the shell ends

Bylaws and articles must say this in hard language: the nonprofit
**cannot** be dissolved for profit of any kind. No founder take. No equity
residual. After debts, either **transfer** remaining assets to another
501(c)(3), or **liquidate** and donate **all** remaining funds to other
nonprofits that qualify. Preferred residual homes (list open; more to be
decided): **Sisters of the Road**, **Food Not Bombs** (or a 501(c)(3) host
for that mission), plus others we name in the open before any wind-down.
Court fallback still only for public-charity purposes — never private
pockets.

## Paths we are weighing

**A · Fiscal sponsorship.** Partner with an existing public charity that
hosts projects. Faster for some donors; their framework; less “our”
entity. Useful as a pilot.

**B · Own public-benefit corporation + federal exemption.** State
formation, governing documents, federal application for tax exemption
under the usual US charity path (often described as 501(c)(3) when
granted). Control and durability; more duty. Only claim after recognition
is real.

**C · Stay informal + diy only.** Cash, Monero, open ledger, no tax
receipts. Purest gut; weakest for foundation grants.

A cooperative is a different tool — useful beside a gift shell, not a
substitute for a public-benefit fund.

## Sequence we are on

1. Keep diy give live at <https://f00.sh/donate> (one pool, quiet rails).
2. Keep the [open ledger](https://f00.sh/ledger) complete.
3. Hold the steward practice and the constitution in public view.
4. Run proposal → open vote → land where anyone can read later.
5. Optionally pilot fiscal sponsorship for one funded project.
6. If we still want our own entity: state formation, bylaws that encode
   this culture, conflict-of-interest policy, EIN, federal application,
   state charity registration where required.
7. Only after recognition: publish status on donate, plan, and ledger —
   still prefer diy over mall rails.

## Purpose language (draft, for later filing)

Educational, literary/artistic, and scientific — not “sell software.”

Create, publish, and freely distribute educational and artistic works —
software, documentation, music, film, visual art, books, magazines, zines
— under open licenses; educate the public about open culture, craft, and
technology; provide materials and infrastructure for that production;
support related open work through a shared program fund.

Activities already in motion: open-source tools under MIT; free digital
media; optional physical editions that recycle cost into more work; public
hub and documentation; open financial ledger.

## What the pool is for

Program spend only: recording and pressing; paper, ink, postage; servers,
bandwidth, domains; materials when they become the next project. Free
digital forever where we control the drop. Physical only as cost-cycle.
No profit distributions. No lifestyle pass-through dressed as “support.”

## Formation checklist (own shell)

- [ ] purpose statement locked to constitution
- [ ] name / DBA availability in home state
- [ ] incorporators + initial board who accept duty
- [ ] articles of incorporation
- [ ] bylaws: stewards, votes, open mandate, no-profit dissolution
- [ ] dissolution: all residual → other 501(c)(3)s (Sisters of the Road, FNB, … TBD)
- [ ] conflict-of-interest policy
- [ ] EIN
- [ ] federal exemption application (when ready)
- [ ] state charity registration if we solicit there
- [ ] books that reconcile to the public ledger
- [ ] grant / gift policies with open deliverables
- [ ] public pages still say the truth about status

Internal worksheets and templates may live offline for counsel review.
Anything that affects the public story belongs on the hub or in git.

## What this plan refuses

Claiming we are a charity before we are. Per-project tip jars. Founder
super-votes. Closed source “for sustainability.” Paywalled members areas.
Discord as the only ballot box. Private books beside a public ledger.
Becoming a pass-through for one person’s lifestyle.

## Status right now

**Not registered.** Collective practice + open ledger + diy give + written
constitution. Working toward a formal shell. No tax receipts. Love first.
